A neighboring world

Meet Mars.

A cold, rocky planet with ancient river valleys, immense volcanoes, and a thin atmosphere. Mars is also one of the most explored places beyond Earth.

01 / Landscape

Ancient terrain

Olympus Mons rises about 22 km above its surroundings, while Valles Marineris stretches thousands of kilometers across the equatorial region.

02 / Atmosphere

Thin, dynamic skies

Carbon dioxide dominates the atmosphere. Dust storms can grow across the planet, and temperatures shift sharply between day and night.

03 / Exploration

Robotic pioneers

Orbiters, landers, and rovers map the planet, study its rocks, and investigate whether ancient Mars could have supported microbial life.

A history of arrival

Milestones

1976
Viking 1 and Viking 2

NASA's first successful Mars landers.

1997
Pathfinder and Sojourner

A lander and the first wheeled rover explored Ares Vallis.

2004
Spirit and Opportunity

Twin rovers began exploring the Martian surface.

2012
Curiosity

The rover landed in Gale Crater to study ancient habitability.

2021
Perseverance and Ingenuity

Exploration of Jezero Crater and powered flight on another planet.

Inner moon

Phobos

Large and close to Mars, Phobos orbits the planet in just over seven hours.

Outer moon

Deimos

Smaller and farther away, Deimos takes about 30 hours to circle Mars.
